# 08 — Moje kampanje („My fundraisers”)

- **URL-ovi:** `/user/whip-rounds/active` (Created by me), `/user/whip-rounds/paid` (Donated by me),
  `/user/whip-round-box` (Donation boxes). U izborniku računa: „My fundraisers”.
- **Grupa:** 3 · korisnik nema vlastitih kampanja, ima jednu donaciju.

## Kartice i filtri

| kartica | brojač | filtri | redoslijed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Created by me | „Active fundraisers 0” | **Active / Disabled / Deleted** | Oldest, Newest, Least collected, Most collected, Least payments, Most payments, Smallest amount available for withdrawal, Largest amount available for withdrawal |
| Donated by me | „Donated by me 1” | – | – |
| Donation boxes | „Donation boxes 0” | Active / Disabled / Deleted | – |

- Prazna kasica: „You have no donation boxes yet.” + „Read more”.

## Kartica doniranog („Donated by me”)

Oznaka „Verified”, naslov, organizator (link na profil), **Created** (datum), **Ends in** (dana), **Goal**,
**Your donation** (iznos i datum), izbornik **More**:

- Open (`/{id}`)
- Create a widget
- Create a tracking link
- Check and manage your donations

## Nije viđeno (vrata)

Izbornik „More” vlastite kampanje (Finances, Notifications, Tracking link, QR, Widget, Poster, Turn off,
Delete) i „Statistics” traže objavljenu kampanju. Vidi pitanje na vrhu `S1b.md`.
